The level

Name it, describe it, and say where the player starts. Everything here shows on the level card and the loading screen.

Identity

The id is how the game and the save file know the level: short, unique, no spaces.

Words

Spawn point

Where the player starts, in metres. One map cell is 2 m, so the centre of cell (8, 8) is (17, 17). You can also click it on the Map.

The world

The generator builds the level from the seed and these settings. Pick a preset in the top bar for a quick start.

Generator

Cuts in the floor

Canyons, crevices and holes, Corruption style. A cut goes through floor and ceiling: keep it at least five metres from the spawn, and give every canyon bridges so the far side stays reachable.

File corruption is the broken-file glitch: blocks of the picture tear, repeat and lose colour.

Light and air

Map

Paint walls and open floor, set the spawn and drop creatures at fixed spots. One cell is 2 m; cell (0, 0) sits at the world origin, x runs right and z runs down. Cells you leave on auto are up to the generator.

Grid

32 × 32 cells = 64 × 64 m

Creatures

Pick one, then click the map to drop it. Drag to move.

Objectives

Stages run in order; each one is a single objective in the HUD. The last stage must be final, or the level never ends.

Stage keys worth knowing

floor which storey the target is on (0 = ground) in mall and apartment levels.
checkpoint elevators, stairs, stair shafts, slides and escalators are respawn checkpoints; untick to turn it off.
endings the bad endings of an unsafe slide: jaws, grinder, whirlpool, drop.
next a level id: the final stage's transport carries you straight into that level.

Creatures

Who is in the level. The lists hold every creature in entities/base/ (any level may use them) plus the ones of this level's pack.

Entities

Spawned when the level starts, at random spots: creature and how many.

Rare spawns

A chance (0 – 1) that one appears.

Placed on the map

Creatures at fixed spots (placed), on top of the ones above. Place them on the Map.

Pack & save

Which pack the level goes in, its file name, and the loot it spawns.

Pack

The id and the file name are kept in step: edit one and the other follows. Once you type a file name of your own, it stays yours.

Loot

Keys, water and batteries found in the level.
